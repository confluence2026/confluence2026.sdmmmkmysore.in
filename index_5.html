About the conference

Sustainability is a confluence of disciplines

The Sustainable Development Goals, adopted by the United Nations in 2015 under the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, comprise 17 interconnected global goals aimed at addressing the world's most pressing challenges — poverty, inequality, climate change, environmental degradation, peace, justice, and sustainable economic growth. Achieving them takes collective effort from governments, academia, industry, civil society, and individuals, and educational institutions have a pivotal role to play in fostering the knowledge, innovation, and leadership needed to advance sustainable development.

Confluence 2026 gathers scholars, researchers and practitioners from Languages & Literature, Commerce, Management, Computer Applications, Physical Sciences and Life Sciences to examine sustainability from six distinct vantage points. Through multidisciplinary dialogue and collaborative learning, the conference aims to inspire meaningful action toward building an inclusive, resilient, and sustainable future for all.

Objectives
  • Provide a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, students, and practitioners to present original work on sustainability.
  • Examine how language, commerce, management, technology, and the sciences individually and jointly advance the SDGs.
  • Encourage cross-disciplinary collaboration between the humanities, social sciences, and natural sciences.
  • Disseminate research findings through quality publications and recognise outstanding scholarship.
  • Strengthen international academic collaboration and exposure for research scholars and academicians.
Sustainability education succeeds only when every discipline claims it as its own. Confluence 2026 is our institution's invitation to scholars everywhere to think, research, and act together for a shared future.
— Prof. N. Bharathi, Principal, MMK & SDM MMV, Organising Chair
Outcomes
  • A multidisciplinary perspective on the UN SDGs, and closer collaboration among academicians, researchers, industry experts and students.
  • An exchange of innovative ideas and best practices that contribute to sustainable and inclusive development.
  • Strengthened academic and institutional collaboration integrating sustainability into teaching, research and community engagement.
SDME Society® & the host institution

MMK & SDM Mahila Mahavidyalaya

SDM Educational Society®, Ujire, is a renowned non-profit educational organisation operating under the esteemed Shree Kshetra Dharmasthala. Guided by Padma Vibhushan Dr. D. Veerendra Heggade, the Society oversees more than 56 premier academic and professional institutions across Karnataka.

MMK & SDM Mahila Mahavidyalaya, Mysuru, affiliated to the University of Mysore, was established in 1990 and has been managed by the SDM Educational Society®, Ujire since 1992. Over three decades the institution has grown into a centre of academic excellence — recognised under Section 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act, and NAAC-accredited with 'B' Grade in 2021. Located on a serene 2.22-acre campus on JLB Road, the college provides a conducive environment for learning.

Venue city

Mysuru — the Cultural Capital of Karnataka

Mysuru, popularly known as the Heritage City, is located about 120 km from Bengaluru and is renowned for its rich history, architectural grandeur, and cultural and educational institutions — including the majestic Mysore Palace, Chamundi Hill, Brindavan Garden, and numerous heritage public buildings and lake ecosystems.

The host institution is strategically located in the heart of Mysuru city, with easy access to major transportation — the Mysuru City Bus Stand and Mysuru Railway Station are within 3–4 km of the college.

Confluence 2026 · Conference tracks

Six streams, one shared destination

Papers and presentations are invited across six tracks. Each track welcomes both conceptual and applied work connecting the discipline to the Sustainable Development Goals.

I

Languages & Literature

"Voices for a Sustainable World"

Languages: Kannada, English, Hindi and Sanskrit. Language and literature have always mirrored a society's relationship with nature — from Vedic hymns to modern regional prose, these traditions carry ecological wisdom, folk knowledge, and calls for social responsibility.

Suggested topics
  • Indian Knowledge Systems for Sustainable Development (Sanskrit)
  • Perspectives on Sustainable Development in Sanskrit Literature
  • Sanskrit Philosophy and Ethics for Sustainable Development
  • Eco-consciousness and nature imagery in Indian regional literature (Kannada, Hindi, Sanskrit, English)
  • Folk and oral traditions as repositories of ecological wisdom
  • Ecocriticism and climate fiction across Indian and global literary traditions

II

Commerce

"Green Ledgers: Financing a Sustainable Economy"

Sustainable commerce sits at the intersection of finance, trade, and responsible growth — equipping students and practitioners with tools to measure, finance, and audit sustainability across circular value chains.

Suggested topics
  • Green accounting and sustainability reporting practices
  • Circular economy models for MSMEs and local businesses
  • Sustainable supply chain management and green logistics
  • ESG investing, green bonds, and sustainable finance instruments
  • Carbon credit trading, emissions markets, and taxation perspectives
  • Data analytics for tracking and auditing corporate sustainability performance
  • Microfinance and financial inclusion for sustainable rural livelihoods
  • Risk analytics for climate-related financial disclosures (TCFD reporting)
III

Management

"Leading with Purpose: Strategy for a Sustainable Enterprise"

Sustainability is no longer a compliance exercise — it is a leadership imperative. This track explores how organisations embed sustainability into strategy, culture, marketing and stakeholder engagement.

Suggested topics
  • Corporate sustainability strategy and green business models
  • Sustainable HR practices and green workplace culture
  • Sustainable marketing and consumer behaviour towards eco-friendly products
  • CSR and stakeholder management for the SDGs
  • Sustainable finance and green investment decision-making in organisations
  • Business analytics and big data for optimising energy and resource use
  • Sustainable tourism management in Karnataka
  • Predictive analytics for supply chain sustainability risk management
IV

Computer Applications

"Smart Technology, Sustainable Tomorrow"

Technology is both a driver of environmental impact and a powerful tool for sustainable solutions — AI, ML, IoT-enabled monitoring, Big Data, Green Computing, Cybersecurity, and Blockchain, powering a more intelligent, secure, digital future.

Suggested topics
  • AI, ML and Generative AI for Sustainable Development
  • NLP and Predictive Analytics for Sustainable Solutions
  • Health, Technology and Sustainability
  • Smart IoT and Sensor Technologies for Environmental Sustainability
  • Big Data and Data Science for Sustainable Decision-Making
  • Edge Computing and IoT for Energy-Efficient Systems
  • Cloud and Green Computing for Sustainable Development
  • Cybersecurity, E-Governance and Blockchain for Sustainable Digital Ecosystems
V

Physical Sciences

"Matter, Energy, and Sustainability" — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics provide the fundamental tools to understand and solve sustainability challenges — from renewable energy generation and storage to green chemical processes and predictive climate models.

Suggested topics
  • Science of clean energy and storage
  • Green chemistry principles for sustainable industrial processes
  • Mathematical dynamics of non-renewable resource depletion
  • Optimization, exponential growth and resource management for sustainable systems
  • Biodegradable polymers and eco-friendly packaging materials
  • Econophysics of sustainability
  • Green Nanotechnology and Nano materials
  • Photovoltaic systems and next-generation solar cell innovations
VI

Life Sciences

Bioremediation to biofuels

Life Sciences hold the key to some of the most promising sustainability solutions — from bioremediation and biofertilizers to climate-resilient crops and biofuels — addressing agriculture, energy, waste management and environmental health.

Suggested topics
  • Microbial bioremediation
  • Genetic engineering, antimicrobial resistance and infectious diseases
  • Biofertilizers, biopesticides, and sustainable agriculture
  • Biofuel production through biotechnological and biochemical innovations
  • AI and machine learning in life sciences
  • Molecular Biology, Drug Discovery, Marine Biology
  • Biomarkers for monitoring environmental pollution
  • Stem cell research and regenerative medicine

Guidelines for full paper submission

  • 3,000–5,000 words, Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 line spacing, A4 with 1-inch margins.
  • Include Title, Author(s) with affiliation, Abstract (250–300 words), Keywords (4–6), Introduction, Body/Methodology, Findings/Discussion, Conclusion and References (APA/MLA/Chicago).
  • Original, unpublished work not under review elsewhere. Similarity index ≤ 20%, AI score ≤ 30% per UGC norms.
  • Maximum of three authors per paper.
  • Email to conference2026@sdmmmkmysore.in, mentioning the track name in the subject line.
  • Selected, presented papers will be published in an international peer-reviewed journal.

Awards & certificates

A Best Paper Award is presented in each of the six tracks, judged on originality, relevance to the SDGs, and quality of presentation. All participants and presenters receive a certificate of participation/presentation.
